# Claim 11.12

Claim 11.12 (Bounded rank cost and chosen coordinates). Let $X \to Y$ be an arrow. A finite tuple $d$ of $Y$ is contained in a subextension $X \to U \to Y$ whose complementary pieces have finite sizes. For an arbitrary bounded tuple $d$, their sizes can be bounded by $\max(|d|, \aleph_0)$. If specified pieces of $Y$ extend those of $X$, the subextension containing $a$ bounded $d$ can be chosen with all its complementary pieces subsets of the specified complementary pieces of $Y$, still of bounded total size.
