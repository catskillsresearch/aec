# Scheme amalgam

From [Theorem 6.6](Theorem_6.6.md).

Theorem 6.6 (Amalgamation and types for a scheme). At sufficiently closed levels, $C$ has amalgamation. If $A \to B_0, B_1$ are arrows and $v_i$ are equally indexed bounded tuples in $B_i$, then an amalgam over $A$ identifying $v_0$ and $v_1$ exists if and only if their diagrams agree on all parameter queries in $A$ of size at most $g(|v_i| + \aleph_0)$.
