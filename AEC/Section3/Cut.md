# Cut

From [§3.1 Finite-support orders and their cuts](3.1.md).

A cut of an order $L$ is a partition $(L^-, L^+)$ with $L^- < L^+$. It is an interior nonprincipal cut when both sides are nonempty, $L^-$ has no greatest element, and $L^+$ has no least element. Its two characters are $\mathrm{cf}(L^-)$ and $\mathrm{ci}(L^+)$. It is balanced if these cardinals are equal. Cofinality and coinitiality here refer to the indicated linear orders.
