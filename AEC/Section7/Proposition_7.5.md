# Proposition 7.5

Proposition 7.5. Let $Y$ be all-dominated over $X + b$ at a sufficiently closed eligible level $\mu$.

1. If an extension $N$ of $X$ contains a tuple $b'$ of the same type over $X$, there is an arrow $Y \to N$ over $X$ sending $b$ to $b'$. The target may be at a larger eligible level. A prescribed bounded tuple match can be retained whenever its joint type with $b$ over $X$ already agrees.

2. Two all-dominated objects over the same data $X + b$ are isomorphic over those data. The isomorphism may retain a prescribed bounded tuple match of the kind just described.

3. In an actual coupling, if a bounded tuple $x$ has product with $b$ over $X$, then $\mathrm{tp}(x/Y)$ is free from $X$.
