# Theorem 14.5

Theorem 14.5 (Categoricity on a tail). If an AEC $K$ is categorical in unboundedly many cardinals, then there is a cardinal $\theta$ such that $K$ is categorical in every cardinal $\kappa \ge \theta$. Here categoricity includes the existence of a model at each such cardinal.
