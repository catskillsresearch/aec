# Finite-support orders

From [Definition 3.1](Definition_3.1.md).

Definition 3.1. For an infinite limit ordinal $\delta$, set

$$\Gamma_\delta = \mathbb{Z}^{(\delta)}, \qquad H(\delta) = \mathbb{Q}^{(\Gamma_\delta)}.$$

Parentheses denote finite support. Both groups are ordered by their greatest differing coordinate. Write $e_\alpha$ for the integer basis vector at $\alpha < \delta$ and $u_g$ for the rational basis vector at $g \in \Gamma_\delta$. The rank of a nonzero vector is its greatest support coordinate. Unless group operations are explicitly mentioned, $H(\delta)$ is used as a pure linear order; in particular $\mathrm{Aut}(H(\delta))$ means its order automorphism group.
