# Proposition 11.7

Proposition 11.7 (Conditional working package). Fix an admissible shape $L$. Suppose that $G_L$ is categorical at every level in an unbounded class of eligible levels closed sufficiently for the preceding constructions. Assume also the eligible-subclass hypothesis of Convention 5.3. On an unbounded further class of sufficiently closed levels, it satisfies the full working package of Definition 5.2, with its actual system isomorphisms and its arrows from Definition 11.1. The resulting diagrams, saturation, and calculus include mixed arrows between eligible levels, and the output class retains the eligible-subclass hypothesis. The closure requirements are uniform in the finite shape and the fixed pointed template. They use only the smaller and larger input levels specified by the general working-package construction.
