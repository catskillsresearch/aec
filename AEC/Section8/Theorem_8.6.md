# Theorem 8.6

Theorem 8.6 (The marked-increment working package). Let $C$ be a working scheme with componentwise arrows and a stationary finite prescription as in Definition 8.1. Assume the eligible-subclass hypothesis of Convention 5.3. On an unbounded further class of sufficiently closed eligible levels, $C^b$ is again a working scheme, with componentwise arrows, fixed template $F^b$, and mixed arrows between these levels. The output class retains the eligible-subclass hypothesis. The added language and bound recipes at this step have set-sized bounds in the input template and prescription. Thus the construction can be performed at each fixed finite depth without class-sized parameters or a language size depending on the level.
