# Lemma 3.7

Lemma 3.7 (Closure of ordinal supports). Let $\theta^+ \le a$ be infinite cardinals and let $\beta$ be an ordinal of cofinality greater than $a$. Every subset $S \subseteq \beta$ of size at most $a$ is contained in a set $T_0 \subseteq \beta$ of size at most $a$ with the following property: for every $P \subseteq T_0$ of size at most $\theta$ and every finite tuple from $\beta$, there is a tuple from $T_0$ having the same order/equality diagram over $P$. Moreover $T_0$ is bounded in $\beta$ and has cofinality greater than $\theta$ in its induced order.
