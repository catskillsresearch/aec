# Corollary 6.7

Corollary 6.7 (Realization into a larger level). Let $\nu < \mu$ be sufficiently closed eligible levels. If $A \to B$ are $\nu$-objects and $A \to N$ is an arrow into a $\mu$-object, then there is an arrow $B \to N$ extending the given arrow on $A$. One may prescribe the image of a tuple of length less than $\nu$ whose diagrams over queries in $A$ below $\nu$ already match. Types of tuples of length less than $\nu$ over $A$ obtained in a larger object are actual $\nu$-types, and the mixed diagram comparisons at those arities agree.
