# Lemma 3.10

Lemma 3.10. If $\kappa$ is an infinite cardinal and $\tau \le \kappa$ is infinite regular, the additively indecomposable ordinals $\delta < \kappa^+$ with $|\delta| = \kappa$ and $\mathrm{cf}(\delta) = \tau$ are cofinal in $\kappa^+$.
