# Proposition 7.8

Proposition 7.8. Let $\lambda$ be a sufficiently closed eligible level, let $X$ be an object there, and let $p = \mathrm{tp}(b/X)$ be a finite-tuple type. For every infinite $\theta < \lambda$ there is a same-level extension $X \le U$ containing $a$ realization of $p$ such that

$\mathrm{tp}(b, c/X)$ is isolated relative to $b$ for every $c \in U$ with $|c| \le \theta$.

More generally the assertion holds for a bounded mark $b$ satisfying Definition 7.7, with $|b| \le \theta$. The closure requirements at the output level are uniform functions of bounded arities; they do not require $\theta$ to have been fixed before the level was chosen.
