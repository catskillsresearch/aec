# Geometric closure

From [Theorem 10.5](Theorem_10.5.md).

Theorem 10.5 (The geometry of the minimal type). On $P(M)$, for every $M_0 \le_K M$ of size at least $\lambda_0$, the preceding finite independence is the independence relation of a finitary pregeometry. Its closure, denoted by $\mathrm{cl}_M$, has the following properties.

1. For finite $F \subseteq P(M)$ and any maximal independent $S \subseteq F$, the set $\mathrm{cl}_M(F)$ consists exactly of those points that do not independently extend $S$. Equivalently, $\mathrm{cl}_M(F)$ is the set of points forced by $F$.
