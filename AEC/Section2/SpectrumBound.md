# Spectrum bound

From [Proposition 2.3](Proposition_2.3.md).

In the bounded case choose the least infinite cardinal $b_c$ strictly above every categoricity cardinal. In the unbounded case choose the least infinite cardinal $b_c$ such that the decoded AEC is categorical in every cardinal at least $b_c$.
