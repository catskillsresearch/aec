# Categorical tail

From [Theorem 1.3](Theorem_1.3.md).

Theorem 1.3 (The qualitative form). An AEC categorical in unboundedly many cardinals is categorical on a tail of the cardinals.

Here “unbounded” means unbounded in the class of all cardinals, and a tail contains every cardinal above some fixed cardinal.
