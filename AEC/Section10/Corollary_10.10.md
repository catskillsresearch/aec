# Corollary 10.10

Corollary 10.10 (No-growth and rank on the entire tail). Increase the threshold $\lambda$ if necessary to include the bounds for the fixed point diagram. In arbitrary cardinalities, if $A \le_K B$ are pointed models with $\|A\| \ge \lambda$ and $P(A) = P(B)$, then $A = B$. Moreover, for every pointed model $M$ with $\|M\| > \lambda$,

$$
|P(M)| = \mathrm{rk}(P(M)) = \|M\|.
$$

The point sets of strong submodels are relatively closed, and relative bases and finite basis supports are available as in Theorem 10.5; no eligibility condition is imposed on these arbitrary cardinalities.
