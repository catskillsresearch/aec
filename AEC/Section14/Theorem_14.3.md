# Theorem 14.3

Theorem 14.3 (Exact lifting at every larger cardinal). For every finite allowed shape and every monotone assignment $(\kappa_s : s \in L)$ of cardinals at least $\gamma$, any prescribed boundary isomorphism between two systems with that assignment extends to an isomorphism of their tops.
