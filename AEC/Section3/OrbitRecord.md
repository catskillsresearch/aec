# Orbit record

From [Lemma 3.6](Lemma_3.6.md).

Fix an infinite tuple length $\theta$. A presentation of a $\theta$-tuple in $E(H(\delta))$ specifies its labels and their finite index lists. Flatten those lists into an indexed sequence of at most $\theta$ points of $H(\delta)$. Its presentation record consists of the labels, the order/equality diagram of this flattened sequence, and all its slot occupancies.
