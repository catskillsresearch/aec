# Lemma 7.11

Lemma 7.11. Suppose $(M_i)_{i \le \delta}$ is an all-dominated-increment chain over $A$ for finite tuples $(b_i)_{i < \delta}$. If a finite tuple $x$ in an actual coupling has product over $A$ with every finite selection of the accumulated $b_i$, then $\mathrm{tp}(x/M_\delta)$ is free from $A$.
