# Lemma 9.5

Lemma 9.5 (Joint diagrams on the boundary). At the levels used for $C_n$, every boundary map between two $n$-axis objects at the same level preserves all bounded $C_n$-diagrams on their boundary unions. This includes joint diagrams of entries from different faces and all equality information on their actual overlaps.
