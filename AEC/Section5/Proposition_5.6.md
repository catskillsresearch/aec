# Proposition 5.6

Proposition 5.6 (The package for single models). At the sufficiently closed levels supplied by Proposition 4.3, the single $K$-models, with strong maps as arrows and the original term presentation $E$, form a working scheme. The levels can be refined to those required in Theorem 4.11 without losing this conclusion.
