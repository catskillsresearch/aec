# The same presentation record

From [Lemma 3.6](Lemma_3.6.md).

For a fixed $\delta$ with $\mathrm{cf}(\delta) > \theta$, equal records give an order automorphism matching the flattened lists by Theorem 3.5. Applying $E$ gives an automorphism of the model matching the tuples. Hence a realized record determines one model orbit.
