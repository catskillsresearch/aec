# Lemma 4.5

Lemma 4.5 (Orbits over parameters). Let $\sigma$ be infinite and suppose

$$
\chi^{\sigma} = \chi, \qquad \chi \ge \ell + 2^{2^{\sigma}}.
$$

At every sufficiently large categoricity cardinal, there are at most $\chi$ automorphism orbits of $\sigma$-tuples over any specified set of at most $\chi$ parameters.
