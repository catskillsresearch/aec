# Additively indecomposable ordinal

From [Lemma 3.8](Lemma_3.8.md).

Lemma 3.8 (Appending a long indecomposable segment). Under the hypotheses of Lemma 3.7, suppose $\beta$ is additively indecomposable. Let $T_0$ be as there, and let $\delta < \beta$ be additively indecomposable, with $\mathrm{otp}(T_0) < \delta$ and $\mathrm{cf}(\delta) > \theta$.

Lemma 3.10. If $\kappa$ is an infinite cardinal and $\tau \le \kappa$ is infinite regular, the additively indecomposable ordinals $\delta < \kappa^+$ with $|\delta| = \kappa$ and $\mathrm{cf}(\delta) = \tau$ are cofinal in $\kappa^+$. Set $\delta = \omega^\rho$. It is additively indecomposable.
