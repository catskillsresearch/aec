# Corollary 9.2

Corollary 9.2 (Lifting an extension of a projection). Under the hypotheses of Proposition 9.1, let $X$ and $Z$ be at the same eligible level, and let $PX \to Z$ be an arrow. There are a $C$-extension $X \to X^{*}$ and a $D$-arrow $Z \to PX^{*}$ whose composite on $P X$ is the projection of $X \to X^{*}$.
