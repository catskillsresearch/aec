# Lemma 7.6

Lemma 7.6. Suppose $A \le B$ are objects at eligible levels $\nu \le \mu$, $|u, c| < \nu$, and the actual type $q_B = \mathrm{tp}(u, c/B)$ is the free lift of $q_A = \mathrm{tp}(u, c/A)$. If $q_B$ is isolated relative to $u$, then $q_A$ is isolated relative to $u$. Here $\nu$ is sufficiently closed for the uniform bounds at $|u, c| + \aleph_0$.
