# Claim 11.11

Claim 11.11 (Free lifts preserve freshness). The free lift of $p^X_s$ along an arrow $X \to V$ is fresh over $V$. Finite jointly fresh tuples have the product of their singleton types. Bounded jointly fresh lists have a unique whole type specified by their labels and admit all-dominated hulls.
