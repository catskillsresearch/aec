# Proposition 4.8

Proposition 4.8 (Uniform testing of placed comparisons). There is a definable nondecreasing infinite cardinal function $g$, depending on $K$ and its fixed presentation, such that the following holds. If $I$ is an infinite well-order, $A = E(I)$, and two placements of a tuple of length at most $\sigma$ have different placed comparisons over $A$, then their natural diagrams already differ over a subset of $A$ of size at most $g(\sigma)$. The bound is independent of $|I|$.
