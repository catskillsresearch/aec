# Theorem 3.5

Theorem 3.5 (Short-sequence order orbits). Let $\delta$ be an infinite limit ordinal and let $\theta$ be an infinite cardinal with $\theta < \mathrm{cf}(\delta)$. Two sequences of length at most $\theta$ in $H(\delta)$ lie in the same $\mathrm{Aut}(H(\delta))$-orbit if and only if they have the same order/equality diagram and the same occupancies of all strict slots over their distinct entries.
