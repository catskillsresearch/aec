# Lemma 7.2

Lemma 7.2. The following hold at sufficiently closed eligible levels.

1. A projection of a relatively isolated type is relatively isolated.

2. Suppose $A \le B, q = \mathrm{tp}(u, c/A)$ is isolated relative to $u$, and an actual coupling over $B$ satisfies

$$
\mathrm{tp}(u/B) = \mathrm{tp}(u/A)^{\mathrm{fr}}_{B}.
$$

Then

$$
\mathrm{tp}(u, c/B) = q^{\mathrm{fr}}_{B}.
$$

Moreover this type is isolated relative to $u$, using the same isolating fragment as $q$. The second assertion also holds when $A$ is at a smaller eligible level than $B$, provided $(u, c)$ and the required uniform bounds are below the level of $A$.
