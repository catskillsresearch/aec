# Coded inclusion

From [Lemma 2.2](Lemma_2.2.md).

To recover the strong relation, require a directed presentation $N = \bigcup D$ of this kind and a nonempty directed subfamily $E \subseteq D$ with $M = \bigcup E$. If $M \le_K N$, use $D = D(N)$ and $E = D(M)$; transitivity makes the second a subfamily of the first.
