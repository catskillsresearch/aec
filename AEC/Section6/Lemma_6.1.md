# Lemma 6.1

Lemma 6.1 (Parameter orbits for a scheme). Fix an infinite $\sigma$. If $\chi > b + \sigma$ and $\chi^\sigma = \chi$, then, at every sufficiently closed level greater than $\chi$, there are at most $\chi$ diagrams of $\sigma$-tuples over a fixed parameter tuple of length at most $\chi$. Moreover, natural diagrams are unchanged by simultaneous disjoint block transpositions: within each of a family of disjoint convex regions, interchange an adjoining block belonging exclusively to the first tuple family with one belonging exclusively to the second, retaining each block's internal order.
