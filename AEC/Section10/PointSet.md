# Point set

From [Definition 10.3](Definition_10.3.md).

Definition 10.3. For $M_0 \le_K M$ with $\|M\| \ge \lambda_0$, let $P(M)$ be the set of realizations of $p_0$ in $M$. More explicitly, $a \in P(M)$ means that in a strong section $N \le_K M$ of size $\lambda_0$ containing $M_0 \cup \{a\}$, the type $\mathrm{tp}(a/M_0)$ is $p_0$. A finite ordered list of distinct points in $P(M)$ is independent if its type over $M_0$, computed in such a section, is the product of the corresponding copies of $p_0$.
