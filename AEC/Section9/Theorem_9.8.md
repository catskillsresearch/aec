# Theorem 9.8

Theorem 9.8 (Exact boundary lifting). For each finite $n$, at further sufficiently closed levels every boundary isomorphism $h : \partial J \to \partial K$ between $C_n$-objects at the same level extends to an isomorphism $J \simeq K$ onto the given target. All the prescribed marks and bounded named data are preserved.
