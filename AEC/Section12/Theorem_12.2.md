# Theorem 12.2

Theorem 12.2 (Exact geometric boundary lifting). For every finite allowed shape, at all sufficiently closed uniform levels, every prescribed boundary isomorphism between two systems of that shape extends to a pointed isomorphism of their tops. The top isomorphism, together with its prescribed restrictions, is an isomorphism of the systems. The refinements of the eligible levels needed for a fixed shape use only the one-dimensional bounds for its working scheme, the bounds for its tiling layouts, and the corresponding assertions for shapes on fewer axes. In particular these refinements have the uniformity specified in Section 13.
