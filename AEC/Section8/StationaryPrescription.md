# Stationary prescription

From [Definition 8.1](Definition_8.1.md).

Definition 8.1 (A stationary prescription and its marked pairs). A stationary finite prescription for $C$ assigns to every object $X$ a type $p_X$ of a finite tuple, with specified component sorts. The assignment commutes with isomorphisms, and, for every arrow $X \to X'$, including an arrow between eligible levels, $p_{X'}$ is the free extension of $p_X$, after identifying the base by that arrow. The prescription is given by fixed set-coded data of the kind used in the applications.
