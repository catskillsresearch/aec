# Largest active label

From [Lemma 11.2](Lemma_11.2.md).

Consequently every $t \subseteq A$ has a largest active subset

$$a_L(t) = \bigcup \{s \in L : s \subseteq t\}.$$

In a geometric system, $X_t = X_{a_L(t)}$ whenever $t$ is inactive.
