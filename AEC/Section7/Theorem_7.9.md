# Theorem 7.9

Theorem 7.9. At sufficiently closed eligible levels, every finite-tuple type $p = \mathrm{tp}(b/X)$ has an all-dominated realization: there is $Y \ge X$ all-dominated over $X + b$ realizing $p$. More generally this holds for a bounded marginal satisfying Definition 7.7. An all-dominated realization embeds over $X, b$ into any actual ambient containing $a$ realization of that type, with the mixed-level conventions of Proposition 7.5.
