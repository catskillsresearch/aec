# Cardinal assignment

From [Definition 14.1](Definition_14.1.md).

Definition 14.1 (Prescribed piece cardinalities). Let $L$ be an allowed family on a finite axis set $a$. A cardinal assignment on $L$ is a family of cardinals

$$(\kappa_s : s \in L), \qquad \gamma \le \kappa_s \le \kappa_t \quad (s \subseteq t \text{ in } L).$$

A system with this assignment satisfies the span and inclusion conditions of Definition 11.1, with a witnessing basis partition $(H_s : s \in L)$ such that $|H_s| = \kappa_s$. The bases are not named. A prescribed boundary isomorphism has the meaning of Definition 12.1.
