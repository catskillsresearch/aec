# Lemma 5.5

Lemma 5.5 (Rich padded orders). Let $\eta$ be infinite. There is a dense linear order $Q_\eta$ without endpoints of cardinality $2^\eta$ such that every cut given by at most $\eta$ parameters on either side is filled. Equivalently, every extension of an order pattern with at most $\eta$ old and at most $\eta$ added positions can be realized over its old positions. If $\mu$ is a strong limit cardinal and $\eta < \mu$, there is a padded order with these properties of cardinality $\mu$. No restriction on $\mathrm{cf}(\mu)$ is needed for this assertion.
