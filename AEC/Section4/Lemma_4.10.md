# Lemma 4.10

Lemma 4.10 (Unique lifts of bounded diagrams). Let $\mu > \ell$ be a strong-limit cardinal of countable cofinality strictly closed below itself under $t$ and $g$. Let $A \le_K B$ have size $\mu$, and identify $A = E(I)$ for a well-order $I$ of size $\mu$. For a tuple $d$ in $B$ of length at most $\sigma < \mu$, the diagrams

$$
D(d, X; B), X \subseteq A, |X| < \mu,
$$

lift to one placed comparison over all of $A$, uniquely as a comparison diagram. Restricting a lifted tuple gives the lift of its restricted diagram.
