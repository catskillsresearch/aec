# Pointed model

From [Proposition 10.6](Proposition_10.6.md).

Choose an enumeration $\bar{m}_0$ of $M_0$ and place it as a strong model in a sufficiently large well-ordered presentation of $K$. Such a placement exists by the order construction and categoricity at $\lambda_0$. Fix the stabilized diagram $\Delta$ of this entire enumeration. The initial supporting positions of this placement will be held fixed in later order presentations.

Proposition 10.6 (Pointed working levels). At all sufficiently closed levels $\mu$ above the bounds for $|\bar{m}_0| = \lambda_0$, models with a distinguished strong copy of $M_0$ whose enumeration has diagram $\Delta$ form a categorical working scheme. Arrows preserve the indicated copy pointwise. For a pointed model $B$ at such a level, $p_0$ has a unique nonalgebraic extension $p_B$ over $B$; it is its free lift.
